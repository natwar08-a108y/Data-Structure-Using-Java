> Divisibility: 
An integer a divides an integer b (written as a | b ) if there exists an integer c such that b = a X c.

> Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic (FTA):
Every integer n > 1 can be represented uniquely as a product of prime numbers, up to the order of the prime factors.

Canonical Form: Any integer n can be written as:

    n = (p1^a1) · (p2^a2) · ... · (pk^ak)

where p1 < p2 < ... < pk are distinct prime numbers, and a_i >= 1.

> Euclidean Algo:
The Euclidean Algorithm is based on the principle that:
gcd (a,b)=gcd (b,a (mod b))
This process is repeated until the remainder becomes 0. The last non-zero remainder is the gcd 

> Extended Euclidean Algorithm 
Bézout's Identity states that if g = gcd(a,b) then there
exist integers x and y such that: 
  ax + by = g
  calculates both gcd(a,b) & coefficients(x,y)

> Mathematical derivation for the Extended Euclidean Algorithm coefficient update:
How it works mathematically (Derivation & Recurrence)

When we compute gcd(a, b), it can be rewritten as:

gcd(a, b) = gcd(b, a mod b)

Let the recursive step be represented as:

b × x₁ + (a mod b) × y₁ = g

Substitute:

a mod b = a − ⌊a/b⌋ × b

Therefore:

b × x₁ + (a − ⌊a/b⌋ × b) × y₁ = g

Rearrange the terms to align with a and b:

a × y₁ + b × (x₁ − ⌊a/b⌋ × y₁) = g

Comparing this with:

a × x + b × y = g

we get our updating relations:

x = y₁

y = x₁ − ⌊a/b⌋ × y₁

Example: Extended Euclidean Algorithm Derivation

Find x and y such that:

30x + 20y = gcd(30, 20)

Step 1: Apply Euclidean Algorithm

gcd(30, 20)
= gcd(20, 30 mod 20)
= gcd(20, 10)
= gcd(10, 20 mod 10)
= gcd(10, 0)

Therefore:

g = 10


Step 2: Consider the recursive result

For:

gcd(b, a mod b)

the recursive result is:

b × x₁ + (a mod b) × y₁ = g

For our example:

a = 30
b = 20
a mod b = 10

So:

20 × x₁ + 10 × y₁ = 10

One valid solution is:

x₁ = 0
y₁ = 1

Therefore:

20(0) + 10(1) = 10


Step 3: Replace a mod b

We know:

a mod b = a - floor(a/b) × b

For our example:

30 mod 20
= 30 - floor(30/20) × 20
= 30 - 1 × 20
= 10

Therefore:

b × x₁ + (a - floor(a/b) × b) × y₁ = g


Step 4: Rearrange

b × x₁ + a × y₁ - floor(a/b) × b × y₁ = g

Group the terms:

a × y₁ + b × (x₁ - floor(a/b) × y₁) = g


Step 5: Compare with the required form

Required:

a × x + b × y = g

We have:

a × y₁ + b × (x₁ - floor(a/b) × y₁) = g

Therefore:

x = y₁

y = x₁ - floor(a/b) × y₁


Step 6: Put values

x₁ = 0
y₁ = 1
floor(30/20) = 1

Therefore:

x = y₁
  = 1

y = x₁ - floor(a/b) × y₁
  = 0 - 1 × 1
  = -1


Step 7: Verify

30x + 20y = 10

30(1) + 20(-1)
= 30 - 20
= 10

Therefore:

x = 1
y = -1

Final result:

30(1) + 20(-1) = 10

gcd(30, 20) = 10
